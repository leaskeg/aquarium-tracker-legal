How to request deletion of your account and data

Last updated: September 20, 2026

You can request deletion of your Aquarium Tracker account and all associated data at any time. We don't currently have a self-serve delete button in the app, so requests are handled by email.

How to request deletion

Send an email to leaskegaming@gmail.com from the same email address you use to sign in to the app, with the subject "Delete my Aquarium Tracker account".

Request account deletion

What gets deleted

Once we verify your request, we'll permanently delete:

  • Your account (name, email, profile info)
  • All tanks, water parameter readings, and maintenance reminders
  • Livestock, equipment, expense, and journal entries
  • Your subscription status record

This happens within 30 days of a verified request.

What we may retain

If you have an active or past Pro subscription, Google Play may retain billing records independently of us, as required for tax, accounting, or legal purposes. We don't control or store your payment details ourselves — Google Play Billing handles those directly.

Questions

See our full Privacy Policy, or email leaskegaming@gmail.com with any questions.
